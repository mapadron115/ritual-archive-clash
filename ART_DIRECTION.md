# Ritual Archive: Clash — original pixel art direction

Owner choice passed on by coordinator 2026-11-14: pixel art for the whole game.

- Source canvas: 640 × 360 for the completed arena/HUD, enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling. A source pixel represents two simulation units. Final page uses `image-rendering: pixelated`.
- Fighter cells: 144 × 128 source pixels, grounded root (64, 120); standing heights span roughly 85–115 source pixels depending on stance and build. All assets use integer rasterization with fully opaque color clusters and transparent background, never a reduction of the cartoon renderer.
- Silhouettes: dark navy one-pixel outline, thicker by overlap in occluded joints; shadowed far limbs; three colors per fabric/skin/hair, one clear technique accent. Lighting comes from the upper left. Avoid scattered lone highlight pixels, gradients, blur and smoothing.
- Animation: discrete authored anticipation, contact and recovery poses, eight advance and eight guarded retreat poses, two idle poses, guard/block, airborne attacks, hit/stun, cast and victory. Gameplay timing chooses the frame; no joint springs or interpolated sprite edges. Feet use the simulation ground, not a new animation collider.
- First fighter: Isolde Varn. Original asymmetrical silver side-sweep, warm face, split navy travel coat, inset ivory shirt, offset cyan clasp, narrow cyan scarf tail and low boots. Her exposed face and angular travel clothes define her own identity.
- Mirror costume: warm pearl coat with mauve shadows, violet trousers and rose accent; silhouette stays the same. Keep P1/P2 overhead tags.
- Roster pass: distinct head shapes, body widths, coat/robe/apron/vest silhouettes, hair, equipment and accents. Shared anatomy is allowed; a palette swap alone does not complete a fighter.
- Stage pass: an original archive courtyard at dusk with architectural depth, stepped cloud clusters, masonry and cloth, a clear foreground fighting plane, and low-contrast distant detail.
- Effects: compact sprite bursts, stepped arcs/rings, bold cores and sparse debris. Preserve Black Flash's dark/red identity and hit-stop without soft glow.
- HUD/menu pass: angular panels, source-pixel borders, bitmap text, consistent native sizing and readable meters.

No existing game's sprites, poses, palettes or stages are used as references or copied assets.

Air normals (v28): punches draw the striking shoulder/fist back with folded knees, then recoil downward and rebuild a suspended guard. Kicks lift and chamber the near knee before the existing extension, fold the shin back on recoil and lower both legs for landing. Six new cells bring each build/costume to 121; the two existing recoil cells are recomposed. Keep the existing contact drawings and exact startup/active/recovery windows. Air basics already retain their attack state until landing; the final pose must continue to read as recovery. No new clock, visual history, control delay, physics or combat change.

Victory (v26): the round winner lowers the guard, lifts a fist, reaches the original raised peak and relaxes into a settled hold. Three new cells bring each build/costume to 112. Existing KO phaseT selects the sequence after the impact pause; only a living, grounded idle winner may celebrate. Attacks, casts, recovery and airborne motion finish first, then join the current round phase. Ties never celebrate; a new round replaces the pose. Match-over holds the settled drawing. Soles meet the floor exactly, including heavy builds. No simulation field, clock, input lock or rule changes.

Voluntary jumps (v27): the existing lifted-knee ascent folds into a tucked crest, unfolds a leg during descent and reaches toward the floor on approach. Three new cells bring each build/costume to 115. Current vertical velocity and floor distance alone select the pose, including slowed jumps and frozen snapshots. Air attacks, airborne hit reactions and Maximum casts retain their own drawing priority. Takeoff, trajectory, air control, attack timing and landing control stay unchanged; do not insert a preparation squat or a landing delay.

Domain distinction (v24): Absolute Counter uses round jade bucklers with thick shaded rims, convex faces and raised diamond bosses on low plinths. Its circular masses distinguish it from Everything I Have's angular emerald cavern. Preserve both established palettes, the sanctuary/cavern ideas, motes and stage floor.

Footwork (v25): eight authored advance cells and eight guarded retreat cells replace the four-cell shared walk. Each holds for three existing active walk ticks. One sole stays at the floor while the swing knee lifts, passes and reaches; the planted sole moves against root travel at frame changes. Advances lean forward with restrained arm counter-motion; retreats lean away with raised hands. Existing signed velocity relative to facing selects the family immediately on reversals/crossings. No extra turn delay or animation state; collision, movement speeds and clocks stay unchanged. Twelve added cells bring each build/costume to 109. Discrete drawing reduces sliding without promising continuous foot locking, especially under speed buffs/slows.

Contact pass (v12): warm directional hit shards, cool cyan guard arcs and wider orange guard-break shards. Two-frame compressed contact, five-frame expansion, then separated fragments; integer mirrored cells keep both directions exact. No soft glow or random frame flicker. Preserve established hit-stop, impact frames and Black Flash identity.

Sound pass (v13): all audio remains original synthesis. A short filtered snap and falling bass body distinguish light/heavy contact; a brighter, shorter pair of tones identifies guard contact. Guard breaks add a falling crack, landings stay quiet, and knockdowns/walls have lower body tones. Black Flash keeps its own dark crack and bass tail. Two-millisecond attacks, shared compression/master gain and capped polyphony control dense effects. Listening on real speakers/headphones remains required.

Silhouette pass (v7): all fifteen idle poses are individually composed. Visible build profiles range from compact/narrow through tall/slender to broad/heavy; shoulders, limbs, stance spread, arm carriage and weight differ. The larger transparent sprite cell leaves room for tall builds, raised fists and the hammer. Simulation hitboxes remain unchanged. Black silhouettes are reviewed separately from color/props.

Animation pass (v9): 49 authored poses per costume, including paired anticipation, attack-specific recoil, settling, launch/fall, curled ground impact, a resting body and two get-up poses. Attack frames follow move time; ground recovery follows the existing down-state timer. Grounded bodies use an exact quarter-turn raster with the lowest opaque pixel on the floor and the body centered at its simulation root. KO bodies stay down. Art contact frames occupy their existing recovery time; no timing, control, damage or collision changes.
