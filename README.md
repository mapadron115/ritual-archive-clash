# Ritual Archive: Clash (demo)

An original pixel-art 2D fighting game demo: fifteen original fighters, each with one technique from the Ritual Archive library. No feats, no levels.

## Run
Double-click `index.html`. Nothing to install. (Online play needs an internet connection on both computers.)

The separate phone-browser edition starts at `mobile/index.html` (v114). Turn the phone sideways. Selection has a page for each fighter and an Arena / moves page, with current choices on the page buttons and Back/Fight below the scrolling content. Move lists use complete names and vertical descriptions with touch instructions. Pause keeps its actions above the list; online guests can change fighters or leave from results while the host controls rematches. Room codes accept Enter without triggering game shortcuts.

Mobile has its own home-screen manifest, standalone launch into `/mobile/`, landscape preference and original CLASH pixel icons (32, 180, 192, 512 and a padded maskable 512). On the hosted HTTPS page, expand **Add to home screen** on the title for Safari and Android browser instructions. Installation menus and orientation support depend on the browser/OS; actual installation and standalone phone launch still need checking. Internet is required to open the game; offline caching is deferred. No install prompt interrupts a match. Rebuild the icons with `node mobile/make-icons.cjs`; the generator uses the game's own bitmap font and palette, with no external assets or dependencies.

The mobile arena fills the available landscape height, with touch controls over its lower corners and notch safe areas respected. Control backgrounds are 40% opaque at rest and brighten while held; labels stay solid. Targets are at least 48 px, with named arts showing CE costs, readiness, denial reasons and cooldown fills. Domain/Maximum shows its name, gauge and once-per-round state. Multiple fingers can hold movement and attack together. Portrait or backgrounding pauses an offline fight until Resume. Online play does not pause locally: guests send a release immediately when leaving the controls, and mobile hosts release guest controls after one second without input packets. A suspended host browser can still stall its authoritative simulation. Returning requires fresh touches; connection loss offers a new host/join attempt. CPU, watch and online modes use the shared game code; desktop remains unchanged. Script checks and generated layout models pass; coordinator measured v108 selection/pause in Chromium, but real phone play and backgrounding still need checking.

## Rules
- Best of three rounds, 99 seconds each. Punch (three presses chain), kick, jump, guard.
- Arts cost Cursed Energy, which refills by itself and faster when you land blows.
- The Domain (or Maximum) can be used once a round, when its gauge is full or you are under 30% health.
  Two Domains meeting cancel each other.
